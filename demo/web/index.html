Analog Gauge Reader

Reads a dial gauge from a photo: a YOLO11s-pose model finds the gauge and seven keypoints (centre, needle tip, scale ends and three points on the scale arc), the scale arc is fitted as a circle seen in perspective, and the needle is read on the straightened dial face. Everything runs in your browser; photos are not uploaded.

Limits. The browser version takes the scale range from the gauge registry (the tags are made-up examples) or from your input; the full version also reads the dial numbers with OCR. A reading is a demonstration, not a safety measurement. When the three readings (upright and turned ±25°) disagree by more than 5% of scale, the answer is RETAKE.
